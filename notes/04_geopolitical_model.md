# Geopolitical and market regimes

Geopolitics will enter as measured state information rather than a collection of discretionary headlines.

A future model may estimate

[
P(S_t mid mathbf X_{0:t}),
]

where (S_t) is an unobserved market regime and (mathbf X_{0:t}) contains market, macroeconomic, and geopolitical observations available by time (t).

Candidate regimes and indicators are hypotheses. They must be defined with timestamped data, tested out of sample, and checked for look-ahead bias. Asset accessibility, liquidity, jurisdiction, currency, and counterparty concentration may also change with the regime.

# Objective and constraints

This chapter will compare possible objective functions and risk constraints, including expected return, probability of reaching a target, volatility, maximum drawdown, Value at Risk, and Conditional Value at Risk.

We will distinguish:

- decision variables: quantities the strategy controls;
- observed state: quantities it measures but cannot control;
- constraints: capital, admissible assets, long-only weights, costs, and risk limits;
- objectives: the return/risk outcome to optimize.

No objective will be selected solely because it produces the most attractive backtest.

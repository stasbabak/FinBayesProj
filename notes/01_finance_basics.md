# Finance basics

This chapter will build the minimum working vocabulary for the project: prices and returns, compounding, volatility, drawdown, correlation, diversification, liquidity, bid/ask spread, market and limit orders, ETFs, bonds, Bitcoin, and custody.

Each concept should include:

1. the practical question it answers;
2. a plain-language explanation;
3. the mathematical definition;
4. a small numerical example;
5. its limitations.
